# Lactofermentation-project
This collaborative project focuses on studying the process of lactofermentation, a natural method of food preservation based on the activity of lactic acid bacteria.
## Definition
Lactofermentation is a natural process in which beneficial bacteria, mainly *Lactobacillus*, convert sugars into lactic acid. This acid acts as a preservative and enhances the flavor, texture, and nutritional quality of foods.

## Principle
The lactic acid lowers the pH of the food, creating an environment that prevents the growth of harmful microorganisms while preserving the food naturally.

## Process
1. Selection and preparation of the raw material (vegetables, milk, etc.)
2. Addition of salt or starter cultures.
3. Fermentation at a controlled temperature.
4. Storage in airtight containers.

## Applications
- Dairy products: yogurt, cheese, kefir.  
- Vegetables: pickles, sauerkraut, kimchi.  
- Beverages: fermented juices.

## Advantages
- Natural preservation method.  
- Improves flavor and texture.  
- Increases digestibility and nutritional value.  
- Promotes gut health.

## Conclusion
Lactofermentation is a sustainable and traditional technique that combines food preservation and health benefits in one natural process.
